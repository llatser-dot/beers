HOUSE RULES
How the pouring works
STEP 1
🎙️

Hold fn & talk

Anywhere on your Mac — Mail, Slack, your editor. Hold the key, say the thing, let go. The words type themselves at your cursor. That's a pour: one pour, one dictation.

STEP 2
🔢

Every word counts

eers keeps a running tally of every word you've served, all on-device. Hit 1,000 and you'll hear the clink — that's a pint in the bank.

STEP 3
🏆

Climb the Pub Wall

Opt in from inside the app and your pints go on the board. Ranks, streaks and bragging rights — first round's on the person in last place.

Brew Controls
Make it yours

One screen. Every control on tap.

Choose the hotkey, where words land, the listening pill position and how much cleanup each pour receives.

  • Clean, Message and Command writing modes
  • Automatic mode follows the active app
  • Local rewrite options with a hard time limit
Little Black Book
Privacy first

Learns on your Mac. Leaves never.

Optional local learning, correction watching and Bouncer shadow predictions — all on-device, all deletable.

  • Pour logs stay in a local file
  • Watch keyboard fixes to get sharper
  • Pour it away whenever you want a clean slate
