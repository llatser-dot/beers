One gesture, any app

A keyboard shortcut for your voice.

Beers stays out of the way until you need it. One hold turns the microphone on; releasing serves the words at your cursor.

1

Hold your pour key

Left Command is the default. Pick another left or right modifier, Fn/Globe or F13 in Brew Controls.

2

Say the thing

The microphone is live while the key is held. Parakeet transcribes speech locally through Core ML.

3

Release to serve

Your polished words paste at the cursor. One dictation is a pour; every 1,000 words pulls a pint.

The honest privacy boundary

Your voice is local by default.

Speech-to-text runs on your Mac and the app has no telemetry. Optional services have separate, explicit boundaries — here they are without the marketing fog.

The Bouncer is shadow-only. Its first two models failed the real-speech gate, so it predicts but does not delete words. It can only activate after DELETE precision reaches at least 0.98 on both the frozen gold set and held-out real dictation.
1

Default dictation stays on the Mac

Audio is transcribed locally. Pour history, vocabulary suggestions and flywheel records live under your user Library.

2

Remote rewriting requires your approval

Loopback Ollama is local. A remote OpenAI-compatible endpoint receives transcript text only after you configure and approve its exact origin.

3

The Pub Wall gets counts, never content

The optional service accepts a handle and aggregate word and pour counts. Cloudflare handles standard request metadata; its schema has nowhere for audio or transcripts.

4

Retraining is a separate operator tool

The app does not run or install the reference loop. If an operator sets it up, selected flywheel text is sent to Anthropic for labelling and may incur their own costs.

Community, when you want it

The Pub Wall is secondary. By design.

Every 1,000 dictated words pulls a pint. The live service is ready to rank opted-in handles without receiving what anyone said.

The native app client is not built yet, so nobody can opt in from Beers today. Until it lands, the honest wall may be rather quiet.
