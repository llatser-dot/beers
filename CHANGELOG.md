# Changelog

Notable public changes are recorded here. Release artefacts and full commit
comparisons remain available on the
[GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/llatser-dot/beers/releases).

## 1.3.2 — 28 July 2026

- Moved vocabulary corrections into the Taproom as **Drunk Slurs**.
- Added correction-driven dictionary suggestions.
- Renamed the settings surface to **Brew Settings**.
- Improved microphone reporting and permissions handling.
- Shipped as an Apple-notarised, stapled and Sparkle-signed app.

## 1.3.1 — 26 July 2026

- First frictionless Apple-notarised public build.
- Standardised new installs on Right Option, Parakeet v3 and Clean writing mode.
- Enabled secure Sparkle updates from the signed appcast.

## 1.3.0 — 22 July 2026

- Matched new installs to the maintainer’s proven daily configuration.
- Kept personal vocabulary, permissions, login state and local learning data out
  of the distributable app.

This was a transitional Apple Development-signed build. Use 1.3.1 or later for
normal public installation.

## 1.2.0 — 17 July 2026

- Added the Doorman permissions walkthrough.
- Added guided Microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring setup.
- Improved AirPods and Bluetooth route-change resilience.

## 1.1.0 — 17 July 2026

- First public Beers release.
- Shipped local Parakeet push-to-talk dictation for Apple silicon Macs.
